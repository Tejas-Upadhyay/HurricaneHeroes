## 2. Email to Lee County Emergency Management

**Send to:** leeeoc@leegov.com  
**Subject:** Free Hurricane Shelter Coordination Tool — Built by Local Student

Hi,

My name is Tejas Upadhyay, and I'm a student at Estero High School in Estero, FL. I built a free web platform called Hurricane Heroes that helps coordinate hurricane relief efforts across Southwest Florida.

The website shows real-time shelter needs during hurricanes — what supplies each shelter needs, quantities, and priorities. It includes:

- 20+ Lee County shelter locations on an interactive map
- Real-time needs tracking (each shelter admin updates what they need)
- Volunteer sign-up for community members
- Donation tracking so donors know exactly what to bring where
- Public help request form for affected residents
- Live NOAA weather alerts

The idea is simple: during a hurricane, shelters struggle to tell the public what they need, and donors don't know where to bring supplies. This platform solves that by connecting them in real time.

I would love the opportunity to show you a quick demo and discuss whether Lee County Emergency Management could use this tool during hurricane season. It's completely free — no cost to the county.

You can view it here: https://southwestfloridahurricaneheroes.com

I'm happy to meet in person, over video call, or answer any questions by email.

Thank you for your time,  
Tejas Upadhyay  
Estero High School  
https://southwestfloridahurricaneheroes.com
